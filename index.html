Questions

Frequently Asked

Are you really open 24 hours?
Yes. A pharmacist and support staff are on duty round-the-clock, every day of the year — including public holidays. Emergency drug needs after midnight are our specialty.
Do you deliver in Ilorin?
Yes. We deliver within Adeta and across most of Ilorin metropolis. Place your order by phone or WhatsApp on 09164515194 and we'll dispatch to you.
How does the free health check work?
Just walk in. There's no appointment, no fee, and no obligation to buy anything. You'll get your blood pressure, random blood sugar and BMI checked, plus a short chat with a pharmacist.
Do you supply hospitals and clinics in bulk?
Absolutely. We are a general supplier of hospital equipment, surgical materials and consumables, with wholesale pricing for hospitals, clinics, NGOs and government facilities. Send a request list on WhatsApp and we'll send a quote.
Can I get a telehealth consultation?
Yes. We arrange video and phone consultations with qualified health professionals. Prescriptions from the consultation can be dispensed straight from Fitmedics and delivered to you.
Do you accept card payments and transfers?
Yes — cash, POS card payment, bank transfer and popular mobile-money options are all accepted at the counter.
